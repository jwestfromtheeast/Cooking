# Sheet-Pan Bibimbap

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Prep:** 10 min · **Cook:** 25 min · **Total:** 35 min
> **Serves:** 4 · **Difficulty:** easy · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1022131-sheet-pan-bibimbap)

> [!note]- Notes
> - Eric Kim's all-at-once take: vegetables roast in quadrants on one pan while rice crisps and eggs oven-fry on another. ([Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nB5voELYrAU))
> - Cold leftover rice works best.
> - Any vegetables on hand work — reduce cook times for delicate ones (spinach, scallions, asparagus).

## Ingredients

#ingredients
- [ ] 6 oz oyster mushrooms, torn into bite-size pieces
- [ ] 1 medium sweet potato (about 6 oz), scrubbed and thinly sliced into half-moons
- [ ] 1 small red onion (about 6 oz), thinly sliced crosswise into half-moons
- [ ] 3 packed cups coarsely chopped Tuscan or curly kale (from 1 small bunch)
- [ ] 6 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] Kosher salt and black pepper
- [ ] 4 cups cooked medium-grain white rice, preferably cold leftovers
- [ ] 4 large eggs
- [ ] 4 tsp toasted sesame oil, plus more for serving
- [ ] 4 tsp gochujang, plus more for serving
- [ ] Kimchi, for serving (optional)

## Directions

1. Position racks in the top and bottom thirds of the oven; heat to 450°F.
2. On a large sheet pan, arrange the mushrooms, sweet potato, red onion and kale in four separate quadrants. Drizzle with 3 tablespoons olive oil, season with salt and pepper, and toss to coat, keeping the vegetables separate and uncrowded (brown, don't steam). Roast on the top rack until the sweet potato is fork-tender, the onion and mushrooms are caramelized, and the kale is crispy but not burnt, 20 to 25 minutes.
3. Meanwhile, heat a second large sheet pan on the bottom rack. In the vegetables' last 5 minutes, remove the hot pan, drizzle with the remaining 3 tablespoons olive oil, spread the rice over half, and crack the eggs onto the other half. Return carefully to the oven and bake until the whites are just set and yolks still runny, 3 to 6 minutes — watch closely.
4. Divide the rice among four bowls, arrange the vegetables in neat piles on top, and slide an egg over each. Drizzle each bowl with 1 teaspoon sesame oil and dollop with 1 teaspoon gochujang (more to taste). Mix everything together before eating; kimchi alongside.

## To Serve

- Stir it all together — that's the "bibim" — with kimchi on the side.

## Variations & Substitutions

- Swap in whatever vegetables need using; this is a fridge-cleanout formula wearing a nice outfit.
