# Gochujang Caramel Cookies

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Prep:** 30 min (incl. 15–20 min chill) · **Bake:** 11–13 min · **Total:** 45 min
> **Makes:** ~8 large cookies · **Difficulty:** easy · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1023675-gochujang-caramel-cookies)

> [!note]- Notes
> - Eric Kim's chewy sugar cookie with snickerdoodle cinnamon and clay-red ripples of gochujang "caramel" (brown sugar + butter mellow the heat).
> - **Mix by hand** — recommended for the most defined crinkles and chewiest texture.
> - Don't overmix the swirl: you want wide, distinct gochujang streaks, not pink dough.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
- [ ] 1/2 cup (8 tbsp) / 115 g unsalted butter, very soft
- [ ] 2 packed tbsp dark brown sugar
- [ ] 1 heaping tbsp gochujang
- [ ] 1 cup / 200 g granulated sugar
- [ ] 1 large egg, at room temperature
- [ ] 1/2 tsp coarse kosher salt (or 3/4 tsp Diamond Crystal)
- [ ] 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon
- [ ] 1 tsp vanilla extract
- [ ] 1/2 tsp baking soda
- [ ] 1 1/2 cups / 185 g all-purpose flour

## Directions

1. In a small bowl, stir together 1 tablespoon of the butter, the brown sugar and gochujang until smooth. Set aside at room temperature.
2. In a large bowl, whisk by hand the remaining 7 tablespoons butter, granulated sugar, egg, salt, cinnamon and vanilla until smooth, about 1 minute. Switch to a spatula and stir in the baking soda, then the flour, gently, to combine. Refrigerate the bowl until the dough is less sticky but still soft and pliable, 15 to 20 minutes.
3. While the dough chills, heat the oven to 350°F and line 2 large sheet pans with parchment.
4. Spoon the gochujang mixture over the dough in 3 to 4 spaced-out blobs. Swirl in long circular strokes so orange-red streaks ripple through the beige — do not overmix.
5. Scoop 1/4-cup rounds at least 3 inches apart (4–5 per pan). Bake until lightly golden at the edges and dry and set in the center, 11 to 13 minutes, rotating pans halfway. Cool completely on the pans — they flatten and finish as they cool. Keeps airtight up to 2 days.

## To Serve

- Slightly warm or room temp; watch people's faces when the heat lands.

## Variations & Substitutions

- More gochujang in the swirl = spicier cookie; the base is a great plain chewy sugar cookie too.
