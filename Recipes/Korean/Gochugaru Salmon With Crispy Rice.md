# Gochugaru Salmon With Crispy Rice

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Prep:** 5 min · **Cook:** 15 min · **Total:** 20 min
> **Serves:** 4 · **Difficulty:** easy · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1022132-gochugaru-salmon-with-crispy-rice)

> [!note]- Notes
> - Get long center-cut salmon fillets for uniform thickness and even cooking.
> - Leftover rice works best for the crispy rice — it toasts in the rendered salmon fat.
> - Gochugaru bloomed in maple syrup, vinegar and butter turns into a mirrored crimson glaze.
> - Balance the richness with fresh, crunchy sides: cucumbers, pickles, or a big green salad.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
- [ ] 4 skin-on salmon fillets (6 oz each)
- [ ] Kosher salt (such as Diamond Crystal) and black pepper
- [ ] 1 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] 4 cups cooked white rice, preferably leftovers
- [ ] 4 tsp gochugaru
- [ ] 2 tbsp maple syrup
- [ ] 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- [ ] 2 tbsp cold, unsalted butter, kept whole
- [ ] Sliced cucumbers or pickles, for serving (optional)

## Directions

1. Season the salmon on all sides with salt and pepper. Heat a large cast-iron or nonstick skillet over medium-high. Add the olive oil and sear the salmon fillets skin side down until the skin is browned and crispy, 2 to 5 minutes. The flesh will turn pale coral as heat creeps up the sides; you want that coral color about two-thirds of the way up for a nice medium-rare. Carefully flip and cook the second side until the flesh feels firm (not wobbly when pressed), another 1 to 2 minutes. Transfer to a plate skin side down; keep the pan with the rendered fat over the heat.
2. Add the rice to the fat in the pan and spread in an even layer, packing it down like a rice pancake. Reduce the heat to medium and cook until the bottom is lightly browned and toasted, about 5 minutes — you should hear it crackle. Flip the rice like a pancake (a spatula helps; it doesn't need to stay in one piece). Cook until lightly toasted on the second side, another 1 to 2 minutes. Go longer for crispier rice, but the crispy-chewy-soft trifecta tastes wonderful.
3. While the rice cooks, stir together the gochugaru, maple syrup, rice vinegar and 1 tsp salt in a small bowl. When the rice is done, divide it among plates. In the now-empty pan, add the gochugaru mixture and cook, stirring constantly, over medium-high until it bubbles up and reduces significantly, 15 seconds to 1 minute — it should look sticky. Turn off the heat and add the cold butter, stirring until fully melted and incorporated. Pour the glaze over the salmon and serve with cucumbers or pickles.

## To Serve

- Crispy rice on the plate, glazed salmon over or beside it, something cold and crunchy alongside.

## Variations & Substitutions

- Any mild chile flake can sub for gochugaru in a pinch, but you'll lose the fruity sweetness that makes the glaze.
