# Momofuku Bo Ssam

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Cure:** 6+ hrs (or overnight) · **Roast:** ~6 hrs + 10–15 min crust · **Total:** ~13 hrs
> **Serves:** 6–10 · **Difficulty:** medium (easy work, long clock) · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/12197-momofukus-bo-ssam)

> [!note]- Notes
> - David Chang's dinner-party sweepstakes winner: salt-sugar cured pork shoulder, slow-roasted until it collapses, finished at 500°F with brown sugar for glistening bark.
> - Its ginger-scallion sauce is a chunkier cousin of our [[Ginger Scallion Sauce]] (adds soy + sherry vinegar, no hot-oil pour).
> - Plan the timeline: cure the night before, roast ~6 hrs, rest up to 1 hr, crust right before serving.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
**Pork**
- [ ] 1 whole bone-in pork butt or picnic ham (8–10 lbs)
- [ ] 1 cup white sugar
- [ ] 1 cup plus 1 tbsp kosher salt
- [ ] 7 tbsp brown sugar

**Ginger-Scallion Sauce**
- [ ] 2 1/2 cups thinly sliced scallions (green and white parts)
- [ ] 1/2 cup peeled, minced fresh ginger
- [ ] 1/4 cup neutral oil (like grapeseed)
- [ ] 1 1/2 tsp light soy sauce
- [ ] 1 scant tsp sherry vinegar
- [ ] 1/2 tsp kosher salt, or to taste

**Ssam Sauce**
- [ ] 2 tbsp fermented bean-and-chili paste (ssamjang)
- [ ] 1 tbsp chili paste (gochujang)
- [ ] 1/2 cup sherry vinegar
- [ ] 1/2 cup neutral oil (like grapeseed)

**Accompaniments**
- [ ] 2 cups plain white rice, cooked
- [ ] 3 heads bibb lettuce, leaves separated, washed and dried
- [ ] 1 dozen or more fresh oysters (optional)
- [ ] Kimchi

## Directions

1. Place the pork in a large, shallow bowl. Mix the white sugar and 1 cup of the salt, then rub the mixture all over the meat. Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate at least 6 hours, or overnight.
2. When ready to cook, heat oven to 300°F. Brush excess sugar mixture off the fat cap and discard any juices. Set the pork in a roasting pan and cook approximately 6 hours, until it collapses and yields easily to a fork — basting hourly with pan juices after the first hour. Rest up to 1 hour if needed.
3. Meanwhile, make the ginger-scallion sauce: combine the scallions with the remaining ingredients in a large bowl, mix well, and adjust salt.
4. Make the ssam sauce: whisk the chili pastes with the vinegar and oil in a medium bowl.
5. Prepare rice, wash lettuce, and shuck oysters if using. Set kimchi and sauces out in serving bowls.
6. When ready to serve, turn oven to 500°F. Stir the remaining 1 tbsp salt with the brown sugar and rub all over the cooked pork. Roast 10 to 15 minutes, until a dark caramel crust forms. Serve hot, tearing meat off the bone at the table and wrapping in lettuce with rice, sauces, and kimchi.

## To Serve

- The whole spread on the table: pork centerpiece, lettuce, rice, both sauces, kimchi, oysters if you're going all out. Everyone builds their own ssam.

## Variations & Substitutions

- Picnic ham works in place of pork butt. Scale sauces up — they always run out first.
