# Cheese Buldak (Fire Chicken)

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Prep:** 10 min · **Cook:** 20 min · **Total:** 30 min
> **Serves:** 4 · **Difficulty:** easy · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1020366-maangchis-cheese-buldak-fire-chicken)

> [!note]- Notes
> - Adapted from Maangchi (Emily Kim), whose [cheese buldak video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T9uI1-6Ac6A) has 7M+ views.
> - Fiery gochugaru-gochujang braised chicken under a cloak of broiler-melted mozzarella. "A child could do it, or an adult who often acts like one."
> - Omit the rice cakes if you can't find them easily.
> - Needs an oven-safe skillet — it goes under the broiler at the end.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
- [ ] 1/4 cup gochugaru (Korean red-pepper flakes)
- [ ] 2 tbsp gochujang (Korean red-pepper paste)
- [ ] 3 tbsp light brown sugar
- [ ] 3 garlic cloves, peeled and minced (about 2 tbsp)
- [ ] 1 (1-inch) piece ginger, minced (about 1 tbsp)
- [ ] 1 tbsp soy sauce
- [ ] 1/2 tsp black pepper
- [ ] 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into 3/4-inch cubes
- [ ] 2 tbsp neutral oil, such as canola or peanut
- [ ] 4 oz sliced Korean rice cakes (optional)
- [ ] 6–8 oz low-moisture mozzarella, thinly sliced
- [ ] 2 scallions, sliced, for garnish

## Directions

1. Combine the gochugaru, gochujang, brown sugar, garlic, ginger, soy sauce and black pepper in a medium bowl and mix well. Add the chicken and stir until well coated.
2. If using rice cakes: swirl the oil into a large, oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat and wait for it to shimmer. Add the rice cakes and cook, turning often, until a little crisp, 3 to 4 minutes. Transfer to a small bowl and set aside. If not using rice cakes, just swirl in the oil and move on.
3. Add the chicken mixture to the pan along with 1/4 cup water. Cover and cook over medium-high heat, stirring occasionally, until the chicken is cooked through, 8 to 10 minutes, adding the rice cakes halfway through if using. Meanwhile, heat the broiler.
4. Remove the chicken from the heat. Cover the pan with the sliced mozzarella, then slide under the broiler. Cook until the cheese has melted and browned in spots, about 2 minutes. Remove, sprinkle with scallions, and serve immediately with rice.

## To Serve

- Straight from the skillet over steamed rice, scallions on top.

## Variations & Substitutions

- Rice cakes (tteok) add chew but are optional.
- Dial the gochugaru down to 2–3 tbsp if 1/4 cup sounds like a dare.
