# Gochujang Buttered Noodles

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Prep:** 5 min · **Cook:** 20 min · **Total:** 25 min
> **Serves:** 4 · **Difficulty:** easy · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1024066-gochujang-buttered-noodles)

> [!note]- Notes
> - Pantry meal, lickety-split: honey and sherry vinegar mellow gochujang's heat into sweet-savory-tangy; butter + pasta water emulsify it into a brick-red sauce.
> - Buy plain gochujang **paste**, not "gochujang sauce" (which has additives). Oil the measuring cup lightly and it slides right out.
> - Single-serving ramen version in Variations.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
- [ ] 1 lb spaghetti or other long pasta
- [ ] 6 tbsp unsalted butter
- [ ] 12 garlic cloves, finely chopped (about 1/3 cup)
- [ ] Salt and pepper
- [ ] 1/4 cup gochujang paste
- [ ] 1/4 cup honey
- [ ] 1/4 cup sherry vinegar or rice vinegar
- [ ] Finely chopped cilantro or thinly sliced scallions (optional)

## Directions

1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Cook the spaghetti according to package instructions. Reserve 1 cup cooking water, drain, and return the pasta to its pot.
2. While the pasta cooks, melt 4 tablespoons of the butter in a skillet over medium-low. Add the garlic, season generously with salt, and cook, stirring occasionally, until it starts to soften but not brown, 1 to 3 minutes.
3. Stir in the gochujang, honey and vinegar; bring to a simmer over medium-high. Cook, stirring constantly, until reduced significantly, 3 to 4 minutes — a spatula dragged across the pan should leave a trail that holds about 3 seconds. Remove from heat.
4. Add the sauce to the pasta pot with the remaining 2 tablespoons butter. Stir vigorously until melted, splashing in pasta water as needed to loosen. Season with salt and pepper, top with cilantro or scallions, and serve immediately.

## To Serve

- Immediately, sauce glossy; scallions or cilantro on top.

## Variations & Substitutions

- **Solo ramen version:** 4–5 oz fresh or instant ramen noodles, 1 1/2 tbsp butter (1 for garlic, 1/2 at the end), 3 garlic cloves, 1 heaping tbsp gochujang, 1 tbsp honey, 1 tbsp vinegar; shave 1–2 minutes off cook times.
- Any long noodle works — this sauce doesn't discriminate.
