# Peruvian Roasted Chicken With Spicy Cilantro Sauce

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Marinate:** 2–12 hrs · **Prep:** 15 min · **Roast:** 35–45 min · **Total:** ~3 hrs (with minimum marinate)
> **Serves:** 4 · **Difficulty:** easy · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1018974-peruvian-roasted-chicken-with-spicy-cilantro-sauce)

> [!note]- Notes
> - The two Peruvian chile pastes carry the dish: **aji amarillo** + **aji panca** (South American markets or online). Pinch subs: sriracha/sambal for amarillo, pasilla powder for panca — less traditional, still tasty.
> - The creamy jalapeño-cilantro-feta sauce is the reason people become obsessed. Make extra.
> - Halving a chicken: shears through the breastbone, flip, cut along the backbone.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
**Chicken**
- [ ] 6 garlic cloves, finely grated or minced
- [ ] 3 tbsp soy sauce
- [ ] 1 tbsp aji amarillo paste (or sriracha/sambal)
- [ ] 1 tbsp lime juice
- [ ] 1 tsp aji panca paste (or pasilla chile powder)
- [ ] 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- [ ] 1 tsp ground cumin
- [ ] 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- [ ] 1/2 tsp fine sea salt
- [ ] 1 (3 1/2–4 1/2 lb) chicken, halved, or 4 lbs bone-in, skin-on parts
- [ ] Extra-virgin olive oil, as needed

**Spicy cilantro sauce**
- [ ] 1 cup cilantro leaves and tender stems
- [ ] 3–4 jalapeños, seeded and diced
- [ ] 1/4 cup / 1 oz crumbled feta
- [ ] 1 garlic clove, chopped
- [ ] 1 1/2 tbsp lime juice, more to taste
- [ ] 2 tsp chopped fresh oregano or basil
- [ ] 3/4 tsp fine sea salt, more to taste
- [ ] 1/2 tsp Dijon mustard
- [ ] 1/2 tbsp aji amarillo or other chile paste
- [ ] 1/2 tsp honey
- [ ] 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- [ ] 1/2 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- [ ] Lime wedges, for garnish

## Directions

1. Whisk together garlic, soy sauce, aji amarillo, lime juice, aji panca, mustard, cumin, pepper and salt in a large bowl.
2. Add the chicken, turning to coat. Cover and refrigerate at least 2 hours and up to 12.
3. Heat oven to 450°F. Remove chicken from marinade, pat dry, arrange skin-side up on a rimmed baking sheet, and drizzle with oil.
4. Roast until the skin is golden and the chicken cooked through, 35 to 45 minutes (pull breasts at 25–35 if using parts). Rest 10 minutes, loosely covered with foil.
5. Meanwhile, blend cilantro, jalapeños, feta, garlic, lime juice, oregano, salt, mustard, chile paste, honey and cumin until smooth. With the motor running, drizzle in the oil to emulsify. Adjust salt and lime.
6. Carve and serve with the sauce and lime wedges.

## To Serve

- With rice, fries, or a simple salad — and the sauce on everything.

## Variations & Substitutions

- Sauce doubles as a dressing, sandwich spread, and vegetable dip.
