# Oven-Roasted Chicken Shawarma

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Marinate:** 1–12 hrs · **Prep:** 15 min · **Roast:** 30–40 min · **Total:** ~1¾ hrs (with minimum marinate)
> **Serves:** 4–6 · **Difficulty:** easy · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1017161-oven-roasted-chicken-shawarma)

> [!note]- Notes
> - The street-side rotisserie classic, oven-ized. One of NYT Cooking's all-time most-rated recipes.
> - Crispiness upgrade: after slicing, sauté the chicken in a screaming-hot pan with a little oil until the edges curl tight.
> - White sauce: plain yogurt cut with mayo, lemon juice and grated garlic. Red: ketchup simmered with crushed red pepper and red-wine vinegar until syrupy — or just hot sauce.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
- [ ] 2 lemons, juiced
- [ ] 1/2 cup plus 1 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] 6 cloves garlic, peeled, smashed and minced
- [ ] 1 tsp kosher salt
- [ ] 2 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- [ ] 2 tsp ground cumin
- [ ] 2 tsp paprika
- [ ] 1/2 tsp turmeric
- [ ] A pinch ground cinnamon
- [ ] Crushed red pepper, to taste
- [ ] 2 lbs boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- [ ] 1 large red onion, peeled and quartered
- [ ] 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley

## Directions

1. Make the marinade: whisk lemon juice, 1/2 cup olive oil, garlic, salt, pepper, cumin, paprika, turmeric, cinnamon and crushed red pepper in a large bowl. Add the chicken, toss to coat, cover, and refrigerate at least 1 hour and up to 12.
2. When ready, heat oven to 425°F. Grease a rimmed sheet pan with the remaining tablespoon of oil. Add the quartered onion to the chicken and marinade, toss once, then spread chicken and onion evenly on the pan.
3. Roast until browned, crisp at the edges, and cooked through, 30 to 40 minutes. Rest 2 minutes, then slice into bits. (For extra crispness, sauté the sliced chicken in a hot oiled pan until it curls tight.)
4. Scatter parsley over the top and serve with tomatoes, cucumbers, pita, white sauce, hot sauce, olives, fried eggplant, feta, rice — anything you desire.

## To Serve

- Build-your-own spread: warm pita, tahini or white sauce, chopped cucumber-tomato salad, olives, feta, hummus swirled with harissa, rice pilaf.

## Variations & Substitutions

- Marinate as long as you can — 12 hours beats 1.
- The white and red sauces from the notes are easy wins; make both.
