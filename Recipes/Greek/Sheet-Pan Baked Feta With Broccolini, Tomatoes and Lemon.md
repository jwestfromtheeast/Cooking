# Sheet-Pan Baked Feta With Broccolini, Tomatoes and Lemon

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Prep:** 10 min · **Roast:** 15–20 min · **Total:** 25 min
> **Serves:** 4 · **Difficulty:** easy · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1021277-sheet-pan-baked-feta-with-broccolini-tomatoes-and-lemon)

> [!note]- Notes
> - Baked feta goes almost creamy — goat-cheese texture with feta tang — while the broccolini crisps, tomatoes burst, and lemon rinds soften.
> - Broccolini stalks are tender; only trim the bottom 1/2 inch.
> - Serve over orzo or farro — or chop everything bite-size and toss it through.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
- [ ] 1 bunch broccolini, ends trimmed, thick stalks split lengthwise (or broccoli, cut bite-size)
- [ ] 1 pint grape tomatoes, halved (about 2 cups)
- [ ] 1 small red onion, peeled, quartered and cut into 2-inch wedges
- [ ] 1 lemon — 1/2 cut into thin rounds, 1/2 left intact for serving
- [ ] 3 tbsp olive oil, plus more for serving
- [ ] 1 tsp ground cumin
- [ ] 1/2 tsp red-pepper flakes
- [ ] Kosher salt and black pepper
- [ ] 2 (6- to 8-oz) blocks feta, cut into 1-inch slices
- [ ] Cooked orzo or farro, for serving
- [ ] 1/2 cup fresh basil or cilantro leaves and fine stems, roughly chopped (optional)

## Directions

1. Heat the oven to 400°F with a rack in the lower third. On a sheet pan, toss the broccolini, tomatoes, onion and lemon slices with the olive oil. Add cumin and red-pepper flakes, season with salt and pepper, and toss again until evenly coated. Nestle the feta slices into the vegetables (a little breaking apart is fine).
2. Roast 15 to 20 minutes, stirring halfway but leaving the feta in place, until the broccolini tips char, the stems pierce easily, and the tomato skins blister and break down.
3. Serve over orzo or farro, drizzled with olive oil, with the reserved lemon half for squeezing and fresh herbs on top.

## To Serve

- Over orzo or farro; the softened lemon rounds are edible and great.

## Variations & Substitutions

- Broccoli works in place of broccolini; try halloumi for a firmer, saltier spin.
