# Crispy Halloumi With Tomatoes and White Beans

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Prep:** 5 min · **Cook:** 25 min · **Total:** 30 min
> **Serves:** 4 · **Difficulty:** easy · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1026520-crispy-halloumi-with-tomatoes-and-white-beans)

> [!note]- Notes
> - Broiled halloumi goes golden-crispy outside, soft and chewy inside, over jammy tomatoes and creamy beans.
> - Any creamy white bean works (cannellini, butter, navy); thyme subs for oregano.
> - Needs an ovenproof pan — stovetop then straight under the broiler.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
- [ ] Olive oil, as needed
- [ ] 1 lb cherry or grape tomatoes, halved lengthwise
- [ ] 2 garlic cloves, minced or pressed
- [ ] 1 tbsp finely chopped parsley, plus more for serving
- [ ] 1 tsp honey, plus more for serving
- [ ] 1/2 tsp dried oregano or thyme
- [ ] Salt and black pepper
- [ ] 1 (15-oz) can cannellini, butter or navy beans, drained
- [ ] 1 (8-oz) block halloumi, cut into 1/4-inch-thick slices
- [ ] 1/2 lemon
- [ ] Crusty bread (optional), for serving

## Directions

1. Set the broiler to high with a rack in the upper third, 3 to 4 inches from the heat.
2. In a large ovenproof pan over medium heat, combine 2 tablespoons olive oil with the tomatoes, garlic, parsley, honey and oregano. Season with salt and pepper and cook, stirring frequently, until the tomatoes soften and release their juices, about 10 minutes.
3. Stir in the beans and heat through, about 3 minutes. Adjust seasoning; turn off the heat.
4. Arrange the halloumi slices on top and broil until golden and crispy, about 5 minutes (broilers vary — watch it).
5. Drizzle generously with olive oil, squeeze the lemon half over, add a light drizzle of honey, garnish with parsley, and serve immediately with bread.

## To Serve

- Straight from the pan with crusty bread; the honey-lemon finish is the move.

## Variations & Substitutions

- Feta (baked, not broiled as long) gets you somewhere similar; halloumi's squeak is the point though.
