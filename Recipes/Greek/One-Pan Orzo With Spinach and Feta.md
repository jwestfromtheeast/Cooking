# One-Pan Orzo With Spinach and Feta

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Prep:** 10 min · **Cook:** 20 min · **Total:** 30 min
> **Serves:** 4 · **Difficulty:** easy · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1021485-one-pan-orzo-with-spinach-and-feta)

> [!note]- Notes
> - Riff on spanakorizo (Greek spinach and rice), with orzo, peas and scallions — a complete meatless meal, or a hearty side for roasted meats or fish.
> - Use vegetable stock to keep it vegetarian.
> - Feta brings creaminess and tang; lemon zest brightens everything.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
- [ ] 2 tbsp unsalted butter
- [ ] 4 large scallions, trimmed and thinly sliced
- [ ] 2 large garlic cloves, minced
- [ ] 8 oz baby spinach leaves (8 cups), coarsely chopped
- [ ] 1 tsp kosher salt
- [ ] 1 3/4 cups low-sodium chicken or vegetable stock
- [ ] 1 cup orzo
- [ ] 1 tsp finely grated lemon zest (from 1 lemon)
- [ ] 3/4 cup crumbled feta (3 oz), plus more for garnish
- [ ] 1/2 cup frozen peas, thawed (optional)
- [ ] 1 cup chopped fresh dill, or parsley or cilantro

## Directions

1. Heat a 10-inch skillet over medium and melt the butter, 30 seconds to 1 minute. Stir in about three-quarters of the scallions (save some greens for garnish) and the garlic; cook, stirring frequently, until softened, about 3 minutes.
2. Stir in the spinach in batches if needed, with 1/2 teaspoon salt. Cook, stirring occasionally, until wilted, about 5 minutes.
3. Stir in the stock and bring to a simmer. Stir in the orzo, lemon zest and remaining 1/2 teaspoon salt. Cover and simmer over medium-low until the orzo is nearly cooked through and most liquid is absorbed, 10 to 14 minutes, stirring once or twice.
4. Stir in the feta, peas (if using) and dill. Cover and cook 1 minute more to finish and warm the peas. Serve sprinkled with more feta and the reserved scallions.

## To Serve

- On its own as dinner, or beside roast chicken or fish.

## Variations & Substitutions

- Parsley or cilantro sub for dill; skip the peas if you're anti-pea.
