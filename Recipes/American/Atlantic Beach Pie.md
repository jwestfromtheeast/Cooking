# Atlantic Beach Pie

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Active:** 55 min · **Chill:** 4 hrs (up to overnight) · **Total:** ~5 hrs
> **Makes:** one 9-inch pie (~8 slices) · **Difficulty:** easy · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1019424-atlantic-beach-pie)

> [!note]- Notes
> - From Bill Smith of Crook's Corner (Chapel Hill, N.C.) — a mash-up of key lime and lemon meringue pie, inspired by the lemon pies at Atlantic Beach seafood restaurants.
> - The salty saltine crust is the whole point. No food processor needed — hands work fine.
> - Top with whipped cream just before serving; flaky sea salt + citrus zest garnish is the Crook's Corner move.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
**Crust**
- [ ] 1 1/2 sleeves saltine (with salt) crackers (about 60 crackers / 200 g)
- [ ] 3 tbsp granulated sugar
- [ ] 1/2 cup / 113 g unsalted butter (1 stick), at room temperature

**Filling**
- [ ] 4 egg yolks
- [ ] 1 (14-oz / 396 g) can sweetened condensed milk
- [ ] 1/2 cup / 120 ml lemon or lime juice, or a mix
- [ ] Pinch of kosher salt
- [ ] Fresh whipped cream, lemon or lime zest, and flaky sea salt, for garnish (optional)

## Directions

1. **Crust:** Heat oven to 350°F. Using a food processor or your hands, pulse or crush the crackers finely — stop before they all turn to dust; some little pieces are OK. Add sugar, then butter.
2. Pulse or work the butter in by hand until the crumbs hold together like dough. Press into and up the sides of a 9-inch pie pan. Freeze for 15 minutes, then bake 12 to 15 minutes, until the crust gets a little color.
3. **Filling:** While the crust cools (it doesn't need to be cold), whisk the egg yolks into the condensed milk in a medium bowl, then whisk in the citrus juice and salt until completely combined.
4. Pour into the shell and bake 14 to 16 minutes, until the filling has set. Refrigerate until completely cold, 4 hours up to overnight. Serve with whipped cream, citrus zest, and a sprinkle of flaky sea salt.

## To Serve

- Cold, with fresh whipped cream, zest, and flaky salt — "a lazy summer's day in pie form."

## Variations & Substitutions

- All lemon, all lime, or any mix of the two juices works.
