# Creamy, Spicy Tomato Beans and Greens

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Prep:** 20 min · **Cook:** 20 min · **Total:** 40 min
> **Serves:** 4 · **Difficulty:** easy · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1025325-creamy-spicy-tomato-beans-and-greens)

> [!note]- Notes
> - Red-pesto energy: cannellini simmered with tomato paste, cream, sun-dried tomatoes and Pecorino, topped with a lemony arugula salad flecked with fried breadcrumbs — crunchy, chewy, crispy, creamy in every bite.
> - Cousin to [[Pesto Beans]] (green edition) — a can of beans made spectacular either way.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
- [ ] 6 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] 2/3 cup panko bread crumbs
- [ ] Salt and black pepper
- [ ] 1 medium yellow onion, minced
- [ ] 4 garlic cloves, minced
- [ ] 1/2 tsp crushed red pepper
- [ ] 1/3 cup tomato paste
- [ ] 2 (14-oz) cans cannellini or other creamy white beans, rinsed
- [ ] 1 cup heavy cream
- [ ] 1/2 cup chopped jarred sun-dried tomatoes in oil
- [ ] 2/3 cup finely grated Pecorino or Parmesan
- [ ] 4 packed cups / 3 oz baby arugula
- [ ] 2 tsp finely grated lemon zest plus 4 tsp juice (from 1 lemon)
- [ ] Toasted bread (optional), for serving

## Directions

1. In a medium skillet, heat 2 tablespoons olive oil over medium. Stir in the panko, season, and cook, stirring and shaking, until toasted and golden, about 3 minutes. Drain on a paper-towel-lined plate; wipe out the skillet.
2. Heat another 2 tablespoons oil over medium. Cook the onion, garlic and crushed red pepper, seasoned, stirring frequently, until softened, about 4 minutes.
3. Add the tomato paste and stir until darkened and combined, about 3 minutes.
4. Stir in the beans, cream, sun-dried tomatoes and 1/3 cup water; simmer, stirring occasionally, until the flavors meld, about 5 minutes. Stir in half the cheese; season to taste.
5. Toss the arugula with the panko, lemon zest and juice, remaining cheese and 2 tablespoons olive oil; season. Pile the salad in the center of the beans and serve with toasted bread.

## To Serve

- Bread mandatory; scoop beans and salad together in each bite.

## Variations & Substitutions

- Butter beans make it lusher; kale (massaged) can stand in for arugula.
