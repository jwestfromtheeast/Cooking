# Dutch Baby

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Prep:** 5–10 min · **Bake:** 25 min · **Total:** 40 min
> **Serves:** 3–4 · **Difficulty:** easy · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/6648-dutch-baby)

> [!note]- Notes
> - Five minutes of work: blend everything, pour into a sizzling buttered skillet, bake. Works for breakfast, brunch, lunch, or dessert.
> - Room-temperature eggs and milk puff better.
> - Watch the butter in the oven — melted, not browned.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
- [ ] 3 large eggs, at room temperature
- [ ] 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- [ ] 1/2 cup whole milk, at room temperature
- [ ] 1 tbsp sugar
- [ ] Pinch of nutmeg
- [ ] 4 tbsp unsalted butter
- [ ] Syrup, preserves, confectioners' sugar or cinnamon sugar, for serving

## Directions

1. Heat oven to 425°F.
2. Combine eggs, flour, milk, sugar and nutmeg in a blender jar and blend until very smooth. (Batter may also be mixed by hand.)
3. Place the butter in a heavy 10-inch skillet and put it in the oven. As soon as the butter has melted (watch that it doesn't burn), add the batter, return the pan to the oven, and bake 20 minutes, until puffed and golden. Lower the oven to 300°F and bake 5 minutes longer.
4. Remove, cut into wedges, and serve at once with syrup, preserves, confectioners' sugar or cinnamon sugar.

## To Serve

- Immediately, while dramatic and puffed — it deflates fast.

## Variations & Substitutions

- Fresh berries and whipped cream; apple slices cooked in butter and sugar; or lightly cooked banana slices dusted with brown sugar.
