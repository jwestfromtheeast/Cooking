# French Onion Grilled Cheese

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Prep:** 5 min · **Cook:** 40 min (mostly caramelizing onions) · **Total:** 45 min
> **Serves:** 2 · **Difficulty:** easy · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1020107-french-onion-grilled-cheese) · [Video](https://youtu.be/zXi2RWY24d0)

> [!note]- Notes
> - Key move: stir the grated Gruyère *into* the warm onions before assembling — even melt, perfect cheese-to-jammy-onion ratio in every bite.
> - Caramelizing takes a solid half hour; onions can be prepped ahead (even slow-cooker).
> - The vinegar deglaze balances the butteriness — or serve with a mustard-vinaigrette side salad instead.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
- [ ] 4 tbsp unsalted butter
- [ ] 1 lb yellow or Vidalia onions, peeled, halved and thinly sliced
- [ ] Kosher salt and black pepper
- [ ] 2 tsp sherry, red-wine or white-wine vinegar (optional)
- [ ] 4 oz Gruyère cheese, grated
- [ ] 4 slices bread, cut no wider than 1/2-inch thick

## Directions

1. In a large skillet over medium-high, melt 2 tablespoons butter. Add the onions, season with salt and pepper, cover, and cook, stirring once or twice, until softened, 3 to 5 minutes. Uncover, reduce to medium-low, and cook, stirring occasionally, until deep golden brown, 20 to 25 minutes — adding a few tablespoons of water and scraping up browned bits if they look dry or near burning. Optionally deglaze with the vinegar and cook until evaporated, ~1 minute. Transfer to a bowl, season, and wipe out the skillet.
2. Stir the cheese into the onions. Lay down two bread slices, scoop half the mixture onto each, top with the remaining slices, and press gently.
3. Melt 1 tablespoon butter in the skillet over medium. Cook the sandwiches until the bottoms are golden, 3 to 4 minutes (lower the heat if browning too fast). Add the last tablespoon of butter, flip, press down, and cook until the cheese is fully melted and the bottoms golden, 3 to 4 minutes more. Serve immediately.

## To Serve

- Hot, halved diagonally (obviously), with a sharp salad or tomato soup.

## Variations & Substitutions

- The onion-cheese-stirring trick works with any melty add-in — bacon, kimchi, mushrooms.
