# Katharine Hepburn's Brownies

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Prep:** 10 min · **Bake:** 30–35 min · **Total:** ~45 min
> **Makes:** 12 brownies · **Difficulty:** easy · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/10782-katharine-hepburns-brownies) · [Video](https://youtu.be/vBySJoleYwM)

> [!note]- Notes
> - Just 1/4 cup flour — that's the secret to the rich, gooey texture. A no-fuss classic, much like its author.
> - **Do not overbake.** Gooey is the goal.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
- [ ] 1/2 cup cocoa
- [ ] 1/2 cup butter (1 stick)
- [ ] 2 eggs
- [ ] 1 cup sugar
- [ ] 1/4 cup flour
- [ ] 1 cup chopped or broken-up walnuts or pecans
- [ ] 1 tsp vanilla
- [ ] Pinch of salt

## Directions

1. Heat oven to 325°F.
2. Melt the butter in a saucepan with the cocoa and stir until smooth. Off heat, cool a few minutes, then transfer to a large bowl. Whisk in the eggs one at a time; stir in the vanilla.
3. In a separate bowl, combine sugar, flour, nuts and salt. Add to the cocoa mixture and stir until just combined.
4. Pour into a greased 8×8-inch pan. Bake 30 to 35 minutes — do not overbake; they should be gooey. Cool, then cut into bars.

## To Serve

- Slightly underdone center, glass of cold milk.

## Variations & Substitutions

- Pecans for walnuts; nuts can be skipped, but Hepburn would disapprove.
