# Chocolate Chip Cookies (Jacques Torres)

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Active:** 45 min · **Chill:** 24–36 hrs (required) · **Total:** ~25 hrs
> **Makes:** 1½ dozen 5-inch cookies · **Difficulty:** medium · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1015819-chocolate-chip-cookies)

> [!note]- Notes
> - THE famous NYT cookie. The 24–36 hr chill is the whole trick — dry ingredients hydrate fully for a firmer dough and a marvelously chewy, chocolate-rich cookie. Dough keeps up to 72 hrs; bake in batches.
> - Don't skimp on chocolate quality (60%+ disks or fèves — Valrhona fèves work). The flat disks layer through the dough instead of sitting as chips.
> - Sea salt on top is not optional.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
- [ ] 2 cups minus 2 tbsp cake flour (8 1/2 oz)
- [ ] 1 2/3 cups bread flour (8 1/2 oz)
- [ ] 1 1/4 tsp baking soda
- [ ] 1 1/2 tsp baking powder
- [ ] 1 1/2 tsp coarse sea salt, plus more for sprinkling
- [ ] 1 1/4 cups unsalted butter (2 1/2 sticks)
- [ ] 1 1/4 cups light brown sugar (10 oz)
- [ ] 1 cup plus 2 tbsp granulated sugar (8 oz)
- [ ] 2 large eggs
- [ ] 2 tsp natural vanilla extract
- [ ] 1 1/4 lbs bittersweet chocolate disks or fèves, at least 60% cacao

## Directions

1. Sift flours, baking soda, baking powder and salt into a bowl. Set aside.
2. Using a mixer with the paddle attachment, cream butter and sugars until very light, about 5 minutes. Add eggs one at a time, mixing well after each. Stir in the vanilla. On low, add the dry ingredients and mix until just combined, 5 to 10 seconds. Drop in the chocolate and incorporate without breaking the pieces. Press plastic wrap against the dough and refrigerate 24 to 36 hours (up to 72).
3. When ready to bake, heat oven to 350°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment or a nonstick mat.
4. Scoop six 3 1/2-oz mounds (generous golf balls) onto the sheet, turning any vertically-poking chocolate pieces horizontal. Sprinkle lightly with sea salt and bake until golden brown but still soft, 18 to 20 minutes. Rest the sheet on a wire rack 10 minutes, then move cookies to another rack. Repeat with remaining dough, or keep it chilled for another day.

## To Serve

- Warm, "with a big napkin."

## Variations & Substitutions

- No cake + bread flour? All-purpose works passably, but the two-flour mix is part of the texture magic.
