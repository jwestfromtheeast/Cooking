# Vinegar Chicken With Crushed Olive Dressing

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Prep:** 10 min · **Roast:** 25–30 min · **Total:** 40 min
> **Serves:** 6 · **Difficulty:** easy · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1020486-vinegar-chicken-with-crushed-olive-dressing)

> [!note]- Notes
> - Tangy, turmeric-stained sheet-pan chicken. The finishing move: the olive-garlic-parsley mixture goes *onto the hot pan* to scrape up the schmaltzy bits — a pan sauce, sheet-pan style.
> - No flipping; skin-side up the whole time.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
- [ ] 3 1/2 lbs bone-in, skin-on chicken parts
- [ ] 1 tsp ground turmeric
- [ ] 6 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] Kosher salt and black pepper
- [ ] 1/2 cup white wine vinegar
- [ ] 1 1/2 cups green Castelvetrano olives, crushed and pitted
- [ ] 2 garlic cloves, finely grated
- [ ] 1 cup parsley, tender leaves and stems, chopped

## Directions

1. Heat oven to 450°F. Place chicken on a rimmed baking sheet, toss with turmeric and 2 tablespoons olive oil, and season with salt and pepper. Arrange skin-side up, pour the vinegar over and around, and put in the oven.
2. Bake without flipping until cooked through and deeply browned, 25 to 30 minutes.
3. Meanwhile, combine olives, garlic, parsley, remaining 4 tablespoons olive oil and 2 tablespoons water in a small bowl; season with salt and pepper.
4. Transfer the cooked chicken to a serving platter, leaving the juices and stuck bits behind on the pan.
5. With the pan on a sturdy surface, pour the olive mixture onto it and gently scrape up all the browned bits, letting it mingle with the rendered fat until saucy. Pour over the chicken and serve.

## To Serve

- With bread, rice, or crispy potatoes to catch the olive dressing.

## Variations & Substitutions

- Any meaty green olive works if Castelvetranos are out; keep them crushed for texture.
