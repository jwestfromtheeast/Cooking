# Golden Diner Pancakes

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Prep:** 15 min · **Cook:** 1½ hrs (incl. 1 hr pre-ferment proof) · **Total:** 1 hr 45 min
> **Serves:** 3–6 (makes 4–6 large pancakes) · **Difficulty:** medium · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1027064-golden-diner-pancakes)

> [!note]- Notes
> - From Sam Yoo of Golden Diner in Manhattan's Chinatown — yeast-risen buttermilk batter cooked in individual skillets for soufflé-pancake height and emoji-perfect roundness.
> - The maple-honey butter and syrup are inspired by Korean honey-butter chips; the syrup's soy sauce is the secret.
> - Nonstick skillets (7–8 inch, 5–6 inch across the bottom) work best — no greasing at all.
> - Butter and compote components can be made ahead; butter keeps refrigerated up to 1 week.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
**Pancake batter**
- [ ] 2 1/4 tsp / 7 g active dry yeast (one 1/4-oz packet)
- [ ] 2 cups / 260 g all-purpose flour, divided
- [ ] 1 1/4 cups / 300 g buttermilk
- [ ] 2 tbsp sugar
- [ ] 3/4 tsp baking soda
- [ ] 1/2 tsp fine salt
- [ ] 2 large eggs
- [ ] 1/4 cup / 60 g canola oil or other neutral oil

**Maple-honey butter**
- [ ] 1/2 cup / 113 g unsalted butter, softened
- [ ] 3 tbsp honey
- [ ] 2 tbsp pure maple syrup
- [ ] 3/4 tsp fine salt

**Maple-honey syrup**
- [ ] 1/2 cup / 113 g unsalted butter
- [ ] 1/3 cup / 100 g honey
- [ ] 1 1/2 tbsp maple syrup
- [ ] 1 tsp soy sauce
- [ ] 1/2 tsp fine salt

**Berry compote**
- [ ] 14 oz / 400 g mixed berries (blueberries, raspberries, stemmed strawberries), cut to the size of the small berries
- [ ] 1/3 cup / 67 g sugar
- [ ] 1 1/2 tsp cornstarch
- [ ] 1 lemon (for zesting)

## Directions

1. **Start the batter:** In a small bowl, whisk the yeast with 1 cup flour. In a small saucepan, heat the buttermilk with 1/4 cup / 60 g water over medium-low, stirring often, until lukewarm (about 100°F), about 5 minutes. Pour the buttermilk into the flour and whisk until smooth. Cover with plastic wrap and let sit for 1 hour to create a pre-ferment — this gives deeper flavor and extra rise.
2. **Maple-honey butter:** In a medium bowl, whisk the butter, honey, syrup and salt until smooth. Keep at room temperature if using within a few hours; otherwise refrigerate airtight up to 1 week (soften and re-whisk before serving).
3. **Maple-honey syrup:** Combine the butter, honey, syrup, soy sauce and salt in a small saucepan. Heat over medium-low, whisking often, until the butter melts completely. While whisking, add 1 1/2 tbsp water. Keep whisking until emulsified, then hold on the lowest heat to keep warm.
4. **Berry compote:** In a large bowl, gently mix the berries, sugar and cornstarch until evenly coated. Heat a large, deep skillet over high until very hot (a drop of water should sizzle away instantly). Add the berry mixture and cook, stirring occasionally, until the blueberries look about to pop, ~2 minutes. Remove from heat.
5. Heat the oven to 350°F with a rack in the center. Set a metal rack in a sheet pan and place it on the center oven rack.
6. **Finish the batter:** After the pre-ferment has proofed 1 hour, whisk the remaining 1 cup flour with the sugar, baking soda and salt in a small bowl. In a large bowl, whisk the eggs and oil until smooth. Scrape the pre-ferment into the egg mixture, then add the dry ingredients. Gently stir until no dry traces remain — lumpy is OK.
7. Heat one or two 7–8 inch nonstick skillets over medium until very hot (no greasing). Fill each pan with batter to ~1/3-inch depth, smoothing to the edges for a round shape. Cook until the bottom is crisp and evenly golden, 2–4 minutes, lowering heat if browning too fast. Flip and cook 1–3 minutes more, tucking in the edges to dome the pancake.
8. Transfer the pancake to the rack in the oven to cook the center through, 2–5 minutes (poke a paring knife in the middle to check for wet batter). Repeat with remaining batter, reheating the pan between pancakes; hold finished ones in the oven.
9. **Serve:** Center one or two hot pancakes per plate and drench immediately with maple-honey syrup. Spoon berry compote on top, then a scoop of maple-honey butter over the berries. Zest the lemon over everything. Serve immediately.

## To Serve

- Optional Golden Diner flourish: quenelle the butter — scoop soft butter with one spoon, then shape it against a second same-size spoon into a football.

## Variations & Substitutions

- Extremely well-seasoned cast iron works in place of nonstick, but nonstick is the move since the pans stay ungreased.
