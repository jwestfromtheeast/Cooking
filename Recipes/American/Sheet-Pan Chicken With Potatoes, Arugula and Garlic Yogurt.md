# Sheet-Pan Chicken With Potatoes, Arugula and Garlic Yogurt

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Marinate:** 30 min (up to 8 hrs) · **Roast:** 40–45 min · **Total:** ~1.5 hrs
> **Serves:** 4 · **Difficulty:** easy · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1017359-sheet-pan-chicken-with-potatoes-arugula-and-garlic-yogurt)

> [!note]- Notes
> - A true one-pan meal, salad included — arugula and dill go on at the end, wilting slightly in the pan's heat.
> - The garlic-yogurt sauce is optional but great; use regular yogurt, not Greek.
> - Doubling? Use two sheet pans — a single crowded layer steams instead of browns.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
- [ ] 1 1/2 lbs chicken thighs and drumsticks
- [ ] 1 1/4 lbs small Yukon Gold potatoes, halved and cut into 1/2-inch slices
- [ ] 2 1/2 tsp kosher salt, more as needed
- [ ] 1/2 tsp black pepper, more as needed
- [ ] 2 tbsp harissa (or another thick hot sauce, such as sriracha)
- [ ] 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- [ ] 4 1/2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, more as needed
- [ ] 2 leeks, white and light green parts, halved lengthwise and thinly sliced
- [ ] 1/2 tsp lemon zest (from 1/2 lemon)
- [ ] 1/3 cup plain yogurt (not Greek)
- [ ] 1 small garlic clove
- [ ] 2 oz baby arugula
- [ ] Chopped fresh dill, as needed
- [ ] Lemon juice, as needed

## Directions

1. Combine chicken and potatoes in a large bowl; season with salt and pepper. Whisk together harissa, cumin and 3 tablespoons oil; pour over and toss. Stand at room temperature 30 minutes, or refrigerate up to 8 hours.
2. Meanwhile, combine leeks, lemon zest, a pinch of salt and the remaining 1 1/2 tablespoons oil in a medium bowl.
3. Heat oven to 425°F. Arrange chicken and potatoes on a large rimmed baking sheet in a single layer. Roast 15 minutes, then toss the potatoes lightly.
4. Scatter the leeks over the pan. Roast until chicken and potatoes are cooked through, golden and slightly crisped, 25 to 30 minutes longer.
5. While the chicken cooks, put the yogurt in a small bowl, grate the garlic over it, and season with salt and pepper.
6. To serve, spoon the yogurt over everything in the pan, scatter with arugula and dill, drizzle with oil and lemon juice, and serve.

## To Serve

- Straight from the pan — sauce, greens, lemon, done.

## Variations & Substitutions

- Sriracha or any thick hot sauce subs for harissa; skip the yogurt sauce if it's not your thing.
