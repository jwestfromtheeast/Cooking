# Lemon Potato Salad With Mint

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Prep:** 20 min · **Cook:** 25 min · **Total:** 45 min
> **Serves:** 8 · **Difficulty:** easy · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1017405-lemon-potato-salad-with-mint)

> [!note]- Notes
> - The anti-mayo potato salad — bright, herbal, with a kick from the chile.
> - Make it the morning you serve it and let it marinate at room temperature all day. Refrigerate for longer storage, but bring back to room temp before serving.
> - Cilantro, parsley, tarragon, or sage can sub for the mint; adjust quantity to taste.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
- [ ] 2 lbs small waxy white or yellow potatoes, roughly the same size
- [ ] Juice of 1 lemon, more for serving
- [ ] 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt, more as needed
- [ ] 1/2 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- [ ] 1/2 cup thinly sliced scallions (white and light green parts), more for serving
- [ ] 1/4 cup torn mint leaves, more for serving
- [ ] 1/4 tsp Turkish pepper, more for serving

## Directions

1. Place whole unpeeled potatoes in a large pot with enough salted water to cover by 1 inch. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat and cook until just tender, 15 to 25 minutes depending on size. Drain and cut into 1 1/2-inch chunks as soon as you can handle them.
2. In a bowl, whisk together lemon juice, salt and olive oil.
3. Transfer the hot potatoes to a large bowl and toss with the dressing, scallions, mint and Turkish pepper. Let cool to room temperature, or refrigerate until ready to use. Just before serving, top with additional lemon juice, scallions, mint and Turkish pepper.

## To Serve

- Room temperature, with a final hit of lemon, scallions, mint, and Turkish pepper.

## Variations & Substitutions

- Aleppo pepper or a mild chile flake stands in for Turkish pepper.
- Herb swaps: cilantro, parsley, tarragon, or sage.
