# Coconut Fish and Tomato Bake

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Prep:** 5 min + 15–30 min marinate · **Cook:** ~15 min · **Total:** ~35 min
> **Serves:** 4 · **Difficulty:** easy · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1022129-coconut-fish-and-tomato-bake)

> [!note]- Notes
> - Coconut-milk dressing with garlic, ginger, turmeric and lime; the broiler turns the tomatoes jammy and their juices enrich the pan sauce.
> - Works with whatever fillets look best: snapper, haddock, striped bass, fluke, sablefish, salmon.
> - The fish comes off the first roast *underdone* on purpose — the broiler finishes it.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
- [ ] 3/4 cup unsweetened coconut milk
- [ ] 1 (1-inch) piece fresh ginger, scrubbed and finely grated
- [ ] 1 garlic clove, finely grated
- [ ] 1/2 tsp ground turmeric
- [ ] 1/2 tsp red-pepper flakes
- [ ] 1 tbsp honey
- [ ] Kosher salt
- [ ] 2 limes
- [ ] 1/2 cup chopped cilantro
- [ ] 4 (6-oz) fish fillets (snapper, haddock, striped bass, fluke, sablefish or salmon), skin on or off
- [ ] 2 pints cherry or grape tomatoes
- [ ] 3 tbsp olive oil

## Directions

1. In a large bowl, whisk together the coconut milk, ginger, garlic, turmeric, red-pepper flakes, honey and 1 teaspoon salt. Zest and juice 1 lime directly into the mixture. Stir in 1/4 cup of the cilantro.
2. Add the fish fillets and turn to coat. Marinate in the refrigerator 15 to 30 minutes.
3. Meanwhile, set one oven rack at lower-middle and another closest to the broiler. Heat oven to 425°F.
4. Place the tomatoes on a large sheet pan, drizzle with 2 tablespoons olive oil, season with salt and toss. Nestle the marinated fish between the tomatoes and spoon all the marinade over the fish; drizzle with the last tablespoon of oil. Roast on the lower-middle rack until the surface is opaque but the center isn't cooked through, 8 to 10 minutes — it should not yet flake. Remove and heat the broiler to high.
5. Broil, rotating once, until the fish is tender and the tomatoes are browning in spots, 5 to 6 minutes. Slice the remaining lime into wedges.
6. Divide fish and tomatoes among dishes, tip the pan juices over, garnish with the remaining cilantro, and serve with lime wedges.

## To Serve

- Sop up the sauce with grilled or toasted baguette, or spoon over steamed rice.

## Variations & Substitutions

- Any medium-firm fillet works; adjust roast time to thickness.
