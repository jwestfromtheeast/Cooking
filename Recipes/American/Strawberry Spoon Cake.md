# Strawberry Spoon Cake

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Prep:** 10 min · **Bake:** 20–25 min (+ brief cooling) · **Total:** ~35 min
> **Serves:** 4 · **Difficulty:** easy · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1021174-strawberry-spoon-cake)

> [!note]- Notes
> - So gooey it's served with a spoon. Frozen or fresh berries both work — thaw frozen ones in the microwave first, and mash well to extract the juice.
> - Optional: ground cardamom or ginger on top before baking, or ribbons of fresh basil after.
> - Vanilla ice cream is "the most important thing."

## Ingredients

#ingredients
- [ ] 1/2 cup / 115 g unsalted butter (1 stick), melted, plus more for greasing
- [ ] 5 oz / 145 g frozen-and-thawed or fresh hulled strawberries (about 1 cup)
- [ ] 2/3 cup / 150 g packed light brown sugar
- [ ] 1/2 cup / 120 ml whole milk, at room temperature
- [ ] 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- [ ] 1 cup / 130 g all-purpose flour
- [ ] 1 tsp baking powder
- [ ] Vanilla ice cream, for serving

## Directions

1. Heat oven to 350°F and grease an 8-inch (square or round) baking dish with butter.
2. Using your hands or the back of a fork, mash the berries to release all their juices; stir in 1/3 cup of the brown sugar. Set aside.
3. In a medium bowl, whisk the melted butter, remaining 1/3 cup brown sugar, milk and salt; add the flour and baking powder and whisk just until smooth. Spread the batter (it's not much) evenly into the dish.
4. Spoon the strawberries and all their juices over the batter. Bake 20 to 25 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean in the center. Cool 3 to 5 minutes, then spoon into bowls and serve warm with ice cream.

## To Serve

- Warm, by the spoonful, with vanilla ice cream melting in.

## Variations & Substitutions

- Other jammy fruit (raspberries, peaches) should work the same way.
