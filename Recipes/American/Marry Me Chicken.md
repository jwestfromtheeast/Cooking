# Marry Me Chicken

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Prep:** 10 min · **Cook:** 50 min · **Total:** 1 hr
> **Serves:** 4 · **Difficulty:** easy · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1024503-marry-me-chicken)

> [!note]- Notes
> - The TikTok-viral one: chicken cutlets in a creamy tomato–sun-dried-tomato sauce, allegedly proposal-inducing.
> - Freezer trick: 20 minutes in the freezer makes breasts much easier to slice into cutlets.
> - Tomato paste is the difference-maker — bright acidity against the rich cream.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
- [ ] 3 large boneless, skinless chicken breasts, or 6 cutlets (about 2 1/4 lbs total), patted dry
- [ ] Kosher salt (such as Diamond Crystal) and black pepper
- [ ] 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
- [ ] 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, plus more as needed
- [ ] 3 tbsp unsalted butter
- [ ] 3 garlic cloves, chopped
- [ ] 1 tbsp tomato paste
- [ ] 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- [ ] Red-pepper flakes, to taste
- [ ] 1 cup low-sodium chicken stock
- [ ] 1/2 to 3/4 cup heavy cream
- [ ] 1/2 cup (1 1/2 oz) grated Parmesan
- [ ] 1/3 cup sliced sun-dried tomatoes, packed in oil
- [ ] Fresh basil, for serving

## Directions

1. If using breasts, slice each in half horizontally from the thickest end for 6 cutlets total. Season both sides well with salt and pepper.
2. Scatter the flour on a large plate, coat the cutlets, and shake off excess. Arrange in a single layer on a sheet pan or plate.
3. Heat the oil in a large pan over medium-high, then reduce to medium and add the butter. Once melted, cook the cutlets until golden on one side, ~5 minutes; flip and cook 4 to 5 minutes more. Work in batches, adding oil as needed. Set the cutlets aside.
4. Reduce heat to low; cook the garlic, stirring often, until fragrant, 1 to 2 minutes. Add tomato paste and stir until the color deepens, ~2 minutes. Add oregano and red-pepper flakes.
5. Raise to medium, add the stock, and simmer, scraping up the browned bits, until reduced by half, ~5 minutes.
6. Add 1/2 cup cream and stir at a gentle simmer until slightly thickened, ~3 minutes, lowering heat as needed. Stir in the Parmesan and sun-dried tomatoes; add more cream if you like, and season.
7. Return the chicken to warm through, ~4 minutes. Off heat, scatter with basil.

## To Serve

- Crusty bread for the sauce and a tangy green salad against the richness — or over pasta, rice, or polenta.

## Variations & Substitutions

- Cutlets from the store skip step 1 entirely; thighs work too with a few extra minutes.
