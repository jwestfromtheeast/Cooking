# Chocolate Lava Cake for Two

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Prep:** 10 min · **Bake:** 12–14 min · **Total:** 25 min
> **Serves:** 2 (one 10-oz ramekin, shared) · **Difficulty:** easy · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1019957-chocolate-lava-cake-for-two)

> [!note]- Notes
> - The Jean-Georges classic, scaled for date night: rich cake and velvety sauce in one.
> - **No chocolate chips** — stabilizers ruin the melt. Use chopped bars or fèves, 70–74%.
> - Make-ahead: batter refrigerates up to a day; add 1–2 minutes bake time from cold.
> - Two 6-oz ramekins instead: bake 7–9 minutes.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
- [ ] 3 oz / 85 g bittersweet chocolate, 70–74% cacao (not chips), chopped (about 1/2 cup)
- [ ] 3 tbsp unsalted butter, cubed, plus more for the ramekin
- [ ] 3 tbsp granulated sugar, plus more for the ramekin
- [ ] 1 large egg
- [ ] 1 large egg yolk
- [ ] 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- [ ] 1/8 tsp kosher salt
- [ ] 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
- [ ] Confectioners' sugar, to serve (optional)

## Directions

1. Heat oven to 425°F. Butter a 10-oz ramekin and dust with granulated sugar.
2. Melt the chocolate and butter in a heat-safe bowl over simmering water (or microwave in 30-second blasts), stirring until smooth. Set aside.
3. Whisk the sugar, egg, yolk, vanilla and salt vigorously until thick, foamy and pale, about 2 minutes. Whisk in the flour until smooth.
4. Fold the chocolate into the egg mixture with a spatula until combined.
5. Pour into the ramekin. Bake 12 to 14 minutes, until the edges are set and puffed but the center is still soft when lightly pressed.
6. Loosen the edges with a small knife, invert onto a plate, lift off the ramekin (oven mitt!), dust with confectioners' sugar, and serve with ice cream or whipped cream.

## To Serve

- Immediately, two spoons, center molten.

## Variations & Substitutions

- A pinch of espresso powder or cayenne in the batter plays well with the chocolate.
