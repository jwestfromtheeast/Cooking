# Cranberry Curd Tart

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Prep:** 30 min · **Cook:** ~60 min · **Total:** 1½ hrs (stages can be spread over days)
> **Serves:** 8–10 · **Difficulty:** medium · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1017817-cranberry-curd-tart)

> [!note]- Notes
> - Tarte au citron energy, but cranberry — and naturally wheat-free thanks to the hazelnut–rice flour crust.
> - Make it in stages: crust and curd both keep 1–2 days ahead; the finished tart keeps a couple of days at room temperature.
> - For the most vibrant color, purée the cooked cranberries before sieving instead of just milling.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
**Hazelnut crust**
- [ ] 1 1/4 cups / 180 g raw hazelnuts
- [ ] 1 cup / 125 g rice flour
- [ ] 1/4 tsp salt
- [ ] 1/2 cup / 112 g sugar
- [ ] 6 tbsp / 85 g softened butter, more as necessary

**Cranberry curd**
- [ ] 12 oz / 340 g cranberries
- [ ] 1 cup / 225 g sugar
- [ ] Peel (orange part only) and juice of 1 orange (about 1/2 cup)
- [ ] 4 oz / 113 g softened butter (1 stick)
- [ ] 2 eggs plus 2 egg yolks

## Directions

1. **Crust:** Heat oven to 325°F. Roast hazelnuts on a baking sheet 10 to 15 minutes, until skins darken and crack. Rub off the skins in a clean towel; discard skins and cool the nuts.
2. In a food processor, grind the nuts with half the rice flour until it resembles coarse cornmeal. Add remaining rice flour and salt; pulse briefly.
3. Cream the sugar and butter in a bowl with a wooden spoon for a minute or two until pale and thick. Add the nut mixture and combine until the dough comes together; if crumbly, add 1–2 tbsp softened butter or a little cold water.
4. Press the dough evenly into a 10-inch tart pan — half for the sides, half for the bottom. Prick the bottom with a fork and freeze 30 minutes (or up to several days).
5. Heat oven to 350°F. Bake the chilled shell about 15 minutes, until lightly brown. Cool.
6. **Curd:** Simmer cranberries, sugar, orange juice and peel in a saucepan over medium heat until the berries have popped and softened, about 10 minutes. Press through a food mill or medium sieve into a bowl (or purée first, then fine-sieve, for the most vibrant color). Whisk the butter into the warm liquid.
7. Lightly beat the eggs and yolks in a bowl. Slowly whisk in a cup of the warm cranberry liquid to temper, then combine everything and whisk together. Return to the pot and cook over low heat until nearly bubbling and thickened, about 10 minutes. Cool to room temperature (or press plastic wrap onto the surface and refrigerate up to 1 day).
8. Pour the cooled curd into the cooled shell and smooth the top. Bake at 350°F for 10 minutes to set. Cool on a rack. Keeps at room temperature up to 2 days.

## To Serve

- Room temperature, maybe with softly whipped cream. A Thanksgiving standout.

## Variations & Substitutions

- The gluten-free crust is a feature, not a compromise — but a standard pâte sucrée works if rice flour is out of reach.
