# Spicy White Bean Stew With Broccoli Rabe

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Prep:** 10 min · **Cook:** 30 min · **Total:** 40 min
> **Serves:** 4 · **Difficulty:** easy · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1020754-spicy-white-bean-stew-with-broccoli-rabe)

> [!note]- Notes
> - Extremely flexible — uses up much of what's on hand. Vegetarian by nature, but can start the pot with sausage, slab bacon, or leftover ham.
> - No harissa? Tomato paste plus a pinch of red-pepper flakes.
> - Kale subs for broccoli rabe; simmer less for brothier soup, more for thicker stew.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
- [ ] 1 large bunch (or 2 small) broccoli rabe or kale, thick stems separated from leaves
- [ ] 1/4 cup olive oil, plus more for drizzling
- [ ] 4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- [ ] 1 medium red or yellow onion, thinly sliced
- [ ] Kosher salt and black pepper
- [ ] 2–3 tbsp harissa or tomato paste
- [ ] Red-pepper flakes (optional)
- [ ] 3 (15-oz) cans large white beans (cannellini, butter, or great Northern), drained and rinsed
- [ ] 4 cups vegetable or chicken broth
- [ ] 1 preserved lemon, thinly sliced, or 1 lemon, halved, for squeezing
- [ ] 2 oz feta or other salty cheese (queso fresco, pecorino), crumbled
- [ ] 1 cup parsley or cilantro, leaves and tender stems
- [ ] Fried or medium-boiled eggs, for serving (optional)

## Directions

1. Tear broccoli rabe or kale leaves into bite-size pieces and set aside. Chop the stems into ~1/4-inch pieces; set aside separately.
2. Heat 1/4 cup olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add garlic and onion, season with salt and pepper, and cook, stirring occasionally, until lightly browned and sizzled at the edges, 4 to 6 minutes.
3. Add harissa (or tomato paste and a pinch of red-pepper flakes) and stir to coat in the oil. Cook until brick red, the sugars start to caramelize, and the oil turns vibrant fiery orange, about 2 minutes.
4. Add beans and season with salt and pepper. Crush a few beans with a wooden spoon to release their creamy interior.
5. Add the broth and reserved stems, season again, and bring to a simmer. Cook to your desired consistency — less time for brothier soup, more for thicker stew — 15 to 20 minutes.
6. Add the leaves and preserved lemon (or lemon juice), stirring to wilt the greens. Season with salt, pepper, and more red-pepper flakes for extra heat.
7. Serve topped with feta and parsley, and eggs if you like.

## To Serve

- A drizzle of olive oil, crumbled feta, herbs, and good bread. A fried egg on top makes it dinner.

## Variations & Substitutions

- Omnivore mode: start the pot with sausage, slab bacon, or leftover ham before the onions.
- Any large white bean works; preserved lemon > fresh if you have it.
