# White Bean Hummus With Tahini and Coriander

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Total:** 5 min, no cooking · **Makes:** 2 1/4 cups · **Difficulty:** easy · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1019521-white-bean-hummus-with-tahini-and-coriander)

> [!note]- Notes
> - Coriander seeds add aroma; the optional miso gives the mellow white beans savory depth.
> - Keeps refrigerated in an airtight container up to 1 week.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
- [ ] 1 garlic clove
- [ ] 1 (15-oz) can white cannellini beans, rinsed and drained
- [ ] 1/4 cup tahini
- [ ] 5 tbsp fresh lemon juice (from 1 large lemon)
- [ ] 2 tsp coriander seeds, coarsely ground, or 1 1/2 tsp ground coriander
- [ ] 2 tsp white miso paste (optional)
- [ ] Kosher salt (such as Diamond Crystal)
- [ ] 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil

## Directions

1. Pulse the garlic in a food processor until minced.
2. Scrape the bowl and add the beans, tahini, lemon juice, coriander, miso (if using) and 1 tsp salt. Pulse until smooth, scraping the bowl occasionally. With the machine running, stream in the olive oil. Season to taste with salt.

## To Serve

- With pita, crackers, or crudités; a drizzle of olive oil and a pinch of ground coriander on top.

## Variations & Substitutions

- Any large white bean works. Skip the miso and it's still great — just a touch less savory.
