# Cottage Cheese Egg Bites

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Prep:** 10 min · **Bake:** 25–30 min · **Total:** 40 min
> **Makes:** 12 egg bites · **Difficulty:** easy · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1026338-cottage-cheese-egg-bites)

> [!note]- Notes
> - Protein-rich make-ahead breakfast: blend just until smooth and frothy (8–10 seconds — overblending = collapse), bake in a water bath for soft, fluffy texture.
> - Silicone muffin pan strongly recommended — they pop right out.
> - Skip the water bath and they're firmer and browned, frittata-style. Still good.
> - Keeps 3 days refrigerated, 3 months frozen; reheat in microwave or 300°F oven.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
- [ ] Nonstick cooking spray
- [ ] 8 large eggs
- [ ] 1 cup (8 oz) full-fat cottage cheese
- [ ] 1 1/2 cups (6 oz) shredded Gruyère, Cheddar, mozzarella or Monterey Jack (or a combo)
- [ ] 1/2 tsp kosher salt (such as Diamond Crystal)
- [ ] 1/4 tsp black pepper
- [ ] Optional mix-ins: raw chopped vegetables (onion, scallions, bell pepper, spinach); cooked vegetables (mushrooms, broccoli, roasted red pepper, potato, squash); cooked bacon or sausage; chopped herbs; hot sauce

## Directions

1. Heat oven to 325°F, rack centered. Bring 3 cups water to a boil and keep simmering. Thoroughly coat a 12-cup muffin pan (silicone or nonstick) with cooking spray.
2. Blend the eggs, cottage cheese, 1 cup of the shredded cheese, salt and pepper on high for 8 to 10 seconds — just until smooth and frothy.
3. Set the muffin pan on a sheet pan. Fill each cup three-quarters full. Add any mix-ins on top and gently stir them in with a chopstick. Top with the remaining 1/2 cup cheese, transfer to the oven, and carefully pour the hot water into the sheet pan to cover the very bottom of the cups.
4. Bake 25 to 30 minutes, until set. Lift the muffin pan off the water bath, rest 5 minutes, and pop out the bites (run a knife around non-silicone cups).

## To Serve

- Warm, a couple at a time, hot sauce nearby.

## Variations & Substitutions

- Treat it as a template: any cheese combo, any leftover cooked vegetables.
