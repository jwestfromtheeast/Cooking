# No-Knead Bread

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Active:** ~30 min · **Rest:** ~20 hrs (12–18 hr ferment + 2 hr rise) · **Bake:** 45–60 min · **Total:** ~21 hrs
> **Makes:** one 1½-lb loaf · **Difficulty:** easy (just slow) · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/11376-no-knead-bread)

> [!note]- Notes
> - One of the most popular NYT recipes ever — Jim Lahey of Sullivan Street Bakery. No kneading, no special equipment; time does all the work.
> - Uses Bittman's updated flour amount (3⅓ cups / 430 g, up from the original 3 cups).
> - Needs a 6–8 qt heavy covered pot (cast iron, enamel, Pyrex, ceramic) preheated in the oven.
> - Kenji's 2021 tweaked version exists if we ever want to compare: [Updated No-Knead Bread](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1022147-updated-no-knead-bread).

## Ingredients

#ingredients
- [ ] 3 1/3 cups / 430 g all-purpose or bread flour, plus more for dusting
- [ ] Generous 1/4 tsp / 1 g instant yeast
- [ ] 2 tsp / 8 g kosher salt
- [ ] Cornmeal or wheat bran, as needed

## Directions

1. In a large bowl combine flour, yeast and salt. Add 1 1/2 cups / 345 g water and stir until blended; dough will be shaggy and sticky. Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and let rest at least 12 hours, preferably about 18, at warm room temperature (~70°F).
2. Dough is ready when the surface is dotted with bubbles. Lightly flour a work surface, place dough on it, sprinkle with a little flour and fold it over on itself once or twice. Cover loosely with plastic and rest about 15 minutes.
3. Using just enough flour to keep the dough from sticking, gently and quickly shape it into a ball. Generously coat a cotton towel (not terry cloth) with flour, bran or cornmeal; put the dough seam side down on the towel, dust with more, cover with a second towel, and let rise about 2 hours — until more than doubled and it doesn't readily spring back when poked.
4. At least 30 minutes before the dough is ready, heat oven to 450°F with a 6–8 qt heavy covered pot inside. Carefully remove the hot pot, and turn the dough into it seam side up (it may look like a mess — that's OK; shake the pan once or twice if uneven). Cover and bake 30 minutes, then uncover and bake another 15 to 30 minutes, until beautifully browned. Cool on a rack.

## To Serve

- Cool fully before slicing (hard, but worth it). Great with the [[Spicy White Bean Stew With Broccoli Rabe]] or [[Pesto Beans]].

## Variations & Substitutions

- Bread flour gives slightly chewier crumb than AP; both work.
