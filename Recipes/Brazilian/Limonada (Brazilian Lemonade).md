# Limonada (Brazilian Lemonade)

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Total:** 10 min · **Serves:** 6 · **Difficulty:** easy · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1024120-limonada-brazilian-lemonade)

> [!note]- Notes
> - Creamy, frosty, tart. "Limonada Suíça" — the condensed milk (marketed by Nestlé in 1940s Brazil) doesn't curdle with the acid and makes it creamy.
> - Made with limes despite the name — *limão* covers both in Portuguese. Blending the whole lime (rind on) adds brightness and depth.
> - **Pulse, don't purée** — pulverized rind turns bitter. Serve immediately.
> - Dairy-free: sweetened condensed coconut milk.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
- [ ] 4 limes, scrubbed and washed, plus wedges or wheels for serving
- [ ] 1/3 cup granulated sugar
- [ ] 7 oz sweetened condensed milk (or sweetened condensed coconut milk for dairy-free)
- [ ] 4 cups cold water
- [ ] 4 cups ice, plus more for serving

## Directions

1. Cut off and discard the tips of the limes, then quarter them.
2. Working in batches if needed, pulse limes, sugar, condensed milk, water and ice in a blender 5 to 8 times, until just combined — extracting flavor without pulverizing the rind. Pieces of rind may still be visible.
3. Strain through a fine-mesh strainer into a pitcher, pressing with the back of a spoon; discard solids.
4. Pour into tall ice-filled glasses, garnish with lime, and serve immediately.

## To Serve

- Immediately, very cold — it separates as it sits.

## Variations & Substitutions

- Condensed coconut milk version is dairy-free and adds a tropical note.
