# Hainanese Chicken and Rice

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Prep:** 15 min · **Cook:** 20 min + 10 min rest · **Total:** ~45 min
> **Serves:** 2 · **Difficulty:** easy · **Source:** [dougdoesdelicious (IG)](https://www.instagram.com/p/DaiwZnvvmjV)

> [!note]- Notes
> - "80/20" version — most of the result without poaching a whole chicken.
> - Bone-in, skin-on thighs preferred.
> - Do NOT skip the two sauces — they're the star of the dish.
> - No need to rinse the rice.
> - See [[#Rice Cooker Variant]] below for the even lazier version.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
**Chicken**
- [ ] 2 chicken thighs (450 g)
- [ ] 2 tsp sesame oil (5 g)
- [ ] 1/4 tsp salt (1.5 g)

**Rice**
- [ ] 1 cup jasmine rice (200 g)
- [ ] 6 cloves garlic (15 g), minced or grated
- [ ] 1/2 inch ginger (15 g), minced or grated
- [ ] 3 green onions (45 g), chopped
- [ ] 1 1/4 cup chicken broth (300 g)
- [ ] 1 tsp chicken bouillon powder (5 g)
- [ ] 1/2 tsp turmeric powder (1 g)

**Ginger Scallion Sauce**
- [ ] 1/2 inch ginger (15 g), minced or grated
- [ ] 1 green onion (15 g), minced
- [ ] 1 tsp sesame oil (5 g)
- [ ] Salt (1 g), to taste
- [ ] 2 tbsp neutral oil (25 g)

**Chili Garlic Sauce**
- [ ] 6 cloves garlic (15 g), minced or grated
- [ ] 1/2 inch ginger (15 g), minced or grated
- [ ] 2 tbsp sambal (25 g)
- [ ] 2 tbsp sriracha (25 g)
- [ ] 1 tbsp chicken broth (15 g)
- [ ] 1 tsp lime juice (5 g)
- [ ] 2 tsp sugar (10 g)

**Toppings (optional)**
- [ ] 1 cucumber (100 g), sliced
- [ ] Cilantro, to taste
- [ ] Green onion, to taste

## Directions

1. Place chicken thighs in a mixing bowl and season with sesame oil and salt.
2. Add jasmine rice, garlic, ginger, green onion, chicken broth, chicken bouillon, and turmeric powder into a pot. (No need to rinse the rice.)
3. Place chicken thighs on top of the rice.
4. Get the pot up to a boil, then drop to a simmer. Cover with a lid and simmer for 20 minutes or until the water completely evaporates.
5. While the chicken and rice cook, prepare the sauces. For the ginger scallion sauce: add ginger, green onion, and salt into a bowl.
6. Heat the sesame oil and neutral oil in a pan until hot — until the oil shimmers or gives its first wisp of smoke. Watch the oil the whole time to avoid a fire.
7. Pour the hot oil into the bowl with the rest of the ginger scallion sauce and mix.
8. For the chili sauce: add garlic, ginger, sambal, sriracha, chicken broth, lime juice, and sugar into a bowl and mix.
9. After 20 minutes, take the chicken out and cut into slices or serve as is. Set the rice aside in the pot to rest for another 10 minutes. Plate a scoop of ginger garlic rice with chicken, the sauces, and optional toppings.

## Rice Cooker Variant

Same dish, ~20% effort ([recipe](https://thefoodie.menu/recipes/rice-cooker-hainanese-chicken-rice) · [IG](https://www.instagram.com/p/C59LsBdSkwr/)). Differences from the one-pot version:

- Scale rice up to **2 rice-cooker cups jasmine rice** (320 g — that's 1.5 standard cups) and **2 cups chicken broth** (480 g); turmeric optional.
- Add rice ingredients to the rice cooker bowl, place seasoned chicken thighs on top, and **run the white rice setting**. No boil/simmer to manage.
- Make both sauces identically while it cooks.
- When the cooker finishes, slice the chicken and plate — no separate rest needed.

## To Serve

- A scoop of the ginger garlic rice, sliced chicken over top, both sauces on the side, cucumber slices, cilantro, and green onion.

## Variations & Substitutions

- Bone-in, skin-on thighs give the best flavor; boneless works and cooks slightly faster.
- Chicken bone broth deepens the flavor over standard broth.
