# The Big Lasagna

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Total:** ~2½ hrs (less with store-bought noodles/sauce) · **Makes:** one 9×13 lasagna (8–12 servings)
> **Difficulty:** hard (a project, but every component can be made a day ahead) · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1021031-the-big-lasagna)

> [!note]- Notes
> - Samin Nosrat's #TheBigLasagna ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GLma4QPNSyg)): handmade pasta, béchamel, tomato sauce, ricotta-spinach filling. Store-bought noodles and marinara are officially sanctioned shortcuts.
> - Ricotta filling and béchamel both keep 1 day ahead, refrigerated — bring to room temp before assembling.
> - Vegan path: cashew-milk béchamel with vegan butter, cashew ricotta, egg-free dried pasta.
> - Freezes well: bake 30 min without browning, cool, freeze up to 4 weeks; finish with mozzarella at 400°F for 25–30 min.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
**Ricotta filling**
- [ ] 4 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil (if using fresh spinach)
- [ ] 1 lb fresh mature or baby spinach, or 1 1/2 cups frozen spinach, thawed
- [ ] Fine sea salt
- [ ] 2 lbs whole-milk or part-skim ricotta (about 4 cups)
- [ ] 6 oz grated mozzarella, provolone or string cheese (2 cups)
- [ ] 3 oz freshly grated Parmesan, Asiago or Grana Padano (1 heaping cup)
- [ ] 20 large fresh basil leaves, finely chopped (~3/4 cup) — or parsley/chives/thyme/marjoram, or 1 tsp dried Italian seasoning

**Béchamel**
- [ ] 1/2 cup unsalted butter (1 stick)
- [ ] 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- [ ] 4 cups whole milk
- [ ] Fine sea salt, black pepper, ground nutmeg (optional)

**Assembly**
- [ ] 4 1/2 cups simple tomato sauce or 32 oz store-bought marinara (preferably no added sugar)
- [ ] Fine sea salt
- [ ] 1 recipe homemade lasagna sheets, 16 fresh store-bought sheets, or 2 (9-oz) packages dried lasagna noodles (or no-boil, boiled to al dente)
- [ ] 1 1/2 oz grated Parmesan (1/2 cup)
- [ ] 3 oz grated mozzarella (1 cup)

## Directions

1. **Filling:** If using fresh spinach, sauté in batches in a hot skillet with 2 tbsp oil and a pinch of salt until stems are tender, ~3 minutes per batch; cool in a single layer. (Frozen: skip to next step.)
2. Combine ricotta, mozzarella, Parmesan, basil and a generous pinch of salt in a large bowl.
3. Squeeze as much water as possible from the cooled/thawed spinach, chop finely, and mix in thoroughly. Adjust salt. (Makes ~6 cups; keeps 1 day refrigerated.)
4. **Béchamel:** Melt the butter in a heavy pot over medium. Whisk in flour, drop heat to very low, and cook ~15 minutes, stirring to prevent browning — it will foam, then look like freshly wet fine sand. Whisking vigorously, slowly pour in milk. Raise to medium and whisk until thickened, ~2 minutes. Season with salt, pepper, nutmeg.
5. Return to low and cook, whisking regularly, 10–15 minutes until thick, smooth, and free of raw-flour taste. Strain or immersion-blend if lumpy (~3½ cups). Press parchment against the surface; set aside. (Keeps 1 day refrigerated.)
6. **Assembly:** Rack ~6 inches below the oven top; heat to 400°F.
7. Bring cold components to room temperature.
8. Boil a large covered pot of water; set a colander in a baking dish near the stove.
9. Salt the water like sea water. Boil pasta sheets 2–3 at a time, ~2 minutes each batch, until floppy and cooked through; drain into the colander and rinse cool. Keep cooking sheets as you assemble.
10. Coat a 9×13 pan (bottom and sides) with ~3/4 cup béchamel.
11. Cover the bottom with 2–3 sheets, minimal overlap, ends hanging over two sides (you'll fold these over the top later).
12. Crumble on ~1/3 of the ricotta mixture; cover with pasta edge to edge (kitchen shears help trim).
13. Spread ~1/3 of the tomato sauce; cover with pasta.
14. Spread ~1 cup béchamel, sprinkle 1/4 cup Parmesan; cover with pasta.
15. Repeat twice more: ricotta → pasta → tomato → pasta → béchamel + Parmesan → pasta. (Thicker noodles: skip a pasta layer as needed to stay below the rim.)
16. Fold the overhanging pasta over a final whole sheet to seal the top; spread with remaining béchamel. Cover with parchment then foil, set on a baking sheet, and bake.
17. After 40 minutes, uncover, sprinkle with the mozzarella, and bake until golden and bubbling, ~20 minutes more.
18. Rest at least 15 minutes before slicing. Leftovers keep 4 days refrigerated.

## To Serve

- With a sharp green salad and red wine; feeds a crowd.
