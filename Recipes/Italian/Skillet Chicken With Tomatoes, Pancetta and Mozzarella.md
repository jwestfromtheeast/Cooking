# Skillet Chicken With Tomatoes, Pancetta and Mozzarella

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Prep:** 10 min · **Cook:** 35 min · **Total:** 45 min
> **Serves:** 4 · **Difficulty:** easy · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1017910-skillet-chicken-with-tomatoes-pancetta-and-mozzarella)

> [!note]- Notes
> - Melissa Clark's "pizza chicken" — chicken Parm energy with succulent bone-in pieces instead of breaded cutlets.
> - Pancetta and anchovies do the flavor heavy-lifting; don't skip the anchovies.
> - Needs an oven-proof skillet: stovetop sear → 400°F oven → broiler finish.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
- [ ] 3 1/2 lbs bone-in chicken pieces (or a 3 1/2 lb chicken cut into 8 pieces)
- [ ] 2 tsp kosher salt
- [ ] 1 tsp black pepper
- [ ] 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- [ ] 5 oz pancetta, diced
- [ ] 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- [ ] 2 anchovy fillets
- [ ] 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
- [ ] 1 (28-oz) can whole plum tomatoes
- [ ] 1 large basil sprig, plus more chopped basil for serving
- [ ] 8 oz bocconcini, halved (or mozzarella cut into 3/4-inch pieces)

## Directions

1. Heat oven to 400°F. Pat chicken dry and season with salt and pepper.
2. In a large oven-proof skillet, warm oil over medium-high heat. Add pancetta and cook, stirring frequently, until browned. Use a slotted spoon to transfer pancetta to a paper-towel-lined plate.
3. Add chicken to skillet. Sear, turning only occasionally, until well browned on all sides, about 10 minutes. Transfer to a large plate. Pour off all but 1 tablespoon oil.
4. Add garlic, anchovy and red pepper flakes to skillet; fry 1 minute. Stir in tomatoes and basil. Cook, breaking up tomatoes with a spatula, until sauce thickens somewhat, about 10 minutes.
5. Return chicken to skillet. Transfer skillet to oven and cook, uncovered, until chicken is no longer pink, about 30 minutes.
6. Scatter bocconcini over skillet. Switch oven to broil. Return skillet and broil until cheese is melted and bubbling, 2 to 3 minutes — watch carefully so it doesn't burn. Garnish with the crisped pancetta and chopped basil before serving.

## To Serve

- Crusty bread or pasta to catch the sauce; the reserved pancetta and fresh basil on top.

## Variations & Substitutions

- Regular low-moisture mozzarella works in place of bocconcini.
