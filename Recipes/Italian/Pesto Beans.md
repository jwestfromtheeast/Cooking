# Pesto Beans

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Prep:** 10 min · **Cook:** 25 min · **Total:** 35 min
> **Serves:** 4 · **Difficulty:** easy · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1025675-pesto-beans)

> [!note]- Notes
> - Part rustic bean stew, part deconstructed pesto — no blender, minimal slicing.
> - Basil and lemon go in at the end so they stay vibrant; the turmeric-sizzled nut oil is the finishing move.
> - Serve with crusty bread or garlic bread.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
- [ ] 8 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] 1/4 cup pine nuts or sliced almonds
- [ ] 1/2 tsp ground turmeric
- [ ] Salt and pepper
- [ ] 3 large shallots, sliced thinly (about 2 cups)
- [ ] 5 garlic cloves, sliced thinly
- [ ] 2 (15.5-oz) cans cannellini beans or other creamy white beans, drained
- [ ] 1 cup vegetable or chicken stock
- [ ] 1 cup finely grated Parmesan (about 4 oz), plus more for serving
- [ ] 1 1/2 cups tightly packed basil leaves, preferably Genovese
- [ ] 1 lemon, cut into wedges, for serving

## Directions

1. Heat 6 tablespoons of the olive oil over medium-low in a 12-inch skillet or small Dutch oven. Add the pine nuts and, once sizzling, stir occasionally until golden brown, about 5 minutes; turn off heat. Stir in the turmeric and season lightly with salt and pepper. Transfer to a small serving bowl.
2. Heat the remaining 2 tablespoons oil over medium-high in the same skillet. Add the shallots and a pinch of salt; cook, stirring, until just softened, about 3 minutes. Stir in the garlic, and when sizzling (about 1 minute), stir in the beans and stock. Bring to a simmer, then turn heat to low.
3. Sprinkle in the cheese in a few additions, stirring vigorously. When it has melted into the broth and the mixture looks creamy, season with salt and turn off the heat. While still hot, tear in the basil leaves (or roughly chop) and stir.
4. Serve hot in bowls, drizzled with the sizzled nut oil, a squeeze of lemon, more black pepper, and extra Parmesan.

## To Serve

- Crusty bread or garlic bread is basically mandatory for the creamy broth.

## Variations & Substitutions

- Sliced almonds sub for pine nuts; any creamy white bean works.
- Vegetable stock keeps it vegetarian.
