#### Intro

Mushroom Crispy Rice has been a longtime fan favorite at our restaurant Majordōmo in Los Angeles. Here we developed a version for a home kitchen. It’s still a showstopper, subbing in some of our Momofuku pantry ingredients. Pro tip: try to find a variety of mushrooms—we used shiitake, maitake, and beech mushrooms—as that will increase the flavors and textures in the recipe. And if you can’t find mushroom broth, regular vegetable broth will work just fine.
