# Mushroom Crispy Rice

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Prep:** 15 min · **Cook:** 75 min · **Total:** ~90 min
> **Serves:** 4 · **Difficulty:** medium · **Source:** [Momofuku](https://shop.momofuku.com/blogs/recipes/miso-mushroom-crispy-rice)

> [!note]- Notes
> - Use a variety of mushrooms (shiitake, maitake, beech) for more flavor and texture.
> - Mushroom broth is ideal; regular vegetable broth works fine.
> - A longtime fan favorite at Majordōmo in LA, adapted for home kitchens.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
- [ ] 2–2 1/2 lbs assorted mushrooms, washed and torn into pieces
- [ ] 3 tbsp + 1/4 cup olive oil
- [ ] 1/4 cup + 2 tbsp white miso
- [ ] 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- [ ] 2 tsp white sugar
- [ ] 1 tsp + 3 tbsp soy sauce
- [ ] 1 scallion, sliced
- [ ] 1 large yellow onion, sliced
- [ ] 6 cloves garlic, sliced
- [ ] 2 cups short grain white rice
- [ ] 1 cup dry white wine
- [ ] 3 1/2 cups mushroom (or vegetable) broth

## Directions

1. Heat oven to 425°F with two racks positioned in the middle.
2. Spread mushrooms across two parchment-lined sheet pans (don't crowd them). Toss each pan with ~2 tbsp olive oil and roast 25–35 minutes, rotating racks halfway, until browned and starting to crisp. Set aside.
3. Make the miso dressing: whisk 1 tbsp olive oil, 2 tbsp white miso, 1 tbsp rice vinegar, 2 tsp sugar, and 1 tsp soy sauce. Set aside.
4. While the mushrooms roast, heat 1/4 cup olive oil in a large oven-safe skillet or braiser over medium-high. Cook onions undisturbed 2 minutes, then lower to medium-low and cook ~8 minutes more until softened and browning. Add garlic, cook 5 minutes, then raise heat to medium-high.
5. Stir in rice and toast 1 minute. Add white wine, scraping up browned bits, and reduce by half (~1 minute). Add broth, remaining 1/4 cup miso, and 3 tbsp soy sauce. Bring to a boil, cover, reduce to low, and simmer 10 minutes.
6. Uncover, gently stir to redistribute liquid, then bake on the middle rack for 15 minutes.
7. Switch broiler to high with a rack in the second slot from the top. Broil the skillet 4–6 minutes, watching closely, until the rice is crisped and deeply browned but not burned.
8. Toss the roasted mushrooms, scallions, and miso dressing together and spoon over the crispy rice.

## To Serve

- Serve straight from the skillet; stir the mushrooms into the rice at the table.

## Variations & Substitutions

- Any mix of hearty mushrooms works — the more variety, the better.
