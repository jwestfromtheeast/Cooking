# Coconut-Miso Salmon Curry

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Prep:** 5 min · **Cook:** 20 min · **Total:** 25 min
> **Serves:** 4 · **Difficulty:** easy · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1020045-coconut-miso-salmon-curry)

> [!note]- Notes
> - The trick: miso is sweated and caramelized *early* with ginger and garlic (instead of whisked in at the end), intensifying its earthy sweetness.
> - The gentle poach works for flaky white fish, shrimp, or scallops too.
> - For heat, garnish with sliced jalapeño or serrano.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
- [ ] 2 tbsp safflower or canola oil
- [ ] 1 medium red onion, halved and sliced 1/2-inch thick (about 2 cups)
- [ ] 1 (1-inch) piece fresh ginger, minced (about 2 tbsp)
- [ ] 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- [ ] Kosher salt and black pepper
- [ ] 1/4 cup white miso
- [ ] 1/2 cup unsweetened, full-fat canned coconut milk
- [ ] 1 (1 1/2-lb) salmon fillet, cut into 2-inch pieces
- [ ] 5 oz baby spinach (about 5 packed cups)
- [ ] 1 tbsp fresh lime juice, plus lime wedges for serving
- [ ] Steamed rice (jasmine or basmati), for serving
- [ ] 1/4 cup chopped fresh basil
- [ ] 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro

## Directions

1. In a large pot, heat the oil over medium. Add onion, ginger and garlic; season with salt and pepper. Cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, about 3 minutes. Add the miso and cook, stirring frequently, until lightly caramelized, about 2 minutes.
2. Add the coconut milk and 3 cups water; bring to a boil over high. Cook until slightly reduced, about 5 minutes.
3. Stir in the salmon, reduce to medium-low, and simmer gently until just cooked through, about 5 minutes. Turn off the heat and stir in the spinach and lime juice.
4. Divide rice among bowls, top with the curry, basil and cilantro, and serve with lime wedges.

## To Serve

- Over jasmine or basmati rice, herbs heaped on, lime squeezed over.

## Variations & Substitutions

- Swap in snapper, cod, shrimp, or scallops — adjust poaching time down for smaller pieces.
