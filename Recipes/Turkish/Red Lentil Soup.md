# Red Lentil Soup

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Prep:** 10 min · **Cook:** 35 min · **Total:** 45 min
> **Serves:** 4 · **Difficulty:** easy · **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1016062-red-lentil-soup)

> [!note]- Notes
> - Based on Turkish mercimek çorbası — light, spicy, bold red (no murky brown). One of NYT Cooking's most-rated recipes ever.
> - Red lentils cook faster than green or black; puréeing half the pot gives the chunky-smooth balance.
> - Lemon at the end is essential against the cumin and chile.
> - Vegetable broth keeps it vegetarian (vegan, even).

## Ingredients

#ingredients
- [ ] 3 tbsp olive oil, plus more for drizzling
- [ ] 1 large onion, chopped
- [ ] 2 garlic cloves, minced
- [ ] 1 tbsp tomato paste
- [ ] 1 tsp ground cumin
- [ ] Salt and black pepper
- [ ] Pinch of chili powder or ground cayenne, plus more to taste
- [ ] 1 quart chicken or vegetable broth
- [ ] 1 cup red lentils
- [ ] 1 large carrot, peeled and diced
- [ ] Juice of 1/2 lemon, more to taste
- [ ] 3 tbsp chopped fresh cilantro

## Directions

1. In a large pot, heat 3 tablespoons oil over high until hot and shimmering. Add onion and garlic; sauté until golden, about 4 minutes.
2. Stir in tomato paste, cumin, 1/4 teaspoon each salt and black pepper, and the chili powder; sauté 2 minutes longer.
3. Add broth, 2 cups water, lentils and carrot. Bring to a simmer, then partly cover and turn heat to medium-low. Simmer until lentils are soft, about 30 minutes. Taste and add salt if needed.
4. Purée half the soup (immersion blender, blender, or food processor) and return it to the pot — it should stay somewhat chunky.
5. Reheat if necessary, then stir in lemon juice and cilantro. Serve drizzled with good olive oil and dusted with chili powder.

## To Serve

- Olive oil drizzle + chili dust on top; warm bread alongside.

## Variations & Substitutions

- Cayenne vs. chili powder changes the heat character; adjust freely and finish with extra lemon.
