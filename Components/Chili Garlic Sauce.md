# Chili Garlic Sauce

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Total:** 5 min, no cooking · **Makes:** enough for 2 servings · **Difficulty:** easy

> [!note]- Notes
> - Sambal + sriracha stand in for the traditional fresh red chilies — the 80/20 shortcut.
> - Keeps ~1 week refrigerated.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
- [ ] 6 cloves garlic (15 g), minced or grated
- [ ] 1/2 inch ginger (15 g), minced or grated
- [ ] 2 tbsp sambal (25 g)
- [ ] 2 tbsp sriracha (25 g)
- [ ] 1 tbsp chicken broth (15 g)
- [ ] 1 tsp lime juice (5 g)
- [ ] 2 tsp sugar (10 g)

## Directions

1. Add everything to a bowl and mix.

## Used In

- [[Hainanese Chicken and Rice]]

Also great on: dumplings, fried rice, grilled meats, noodle soups, eggs.
