# Ginger Scallion Sauce

> [!info]+ At a Glance
> **Total:** 10 min · **Makes:** enough for 2 servings · **Difficulty:** easy

> [!note]- Notes
> - The hot-oil pour is what blooms the aromatics — don't skip it.
> - Scales up easily; keeps ~1 week refrigerated.

## Ingredients

#ingredients
- [ ] 1/2 inch ginger (15 g), minced or grated
- [ ] 1 green onion (15 g), minced
- [ ] 1 tsp sesame oil (5 g)
- [ ] Salt (1 g), to taste
- [ ] 2 tbsp neutral oil (25 g)

## Directions

1. Add ginger, green onion, and salt into a heatproof bowl.
2. Heat the sesame oil and neutral oil in a pan until hot — shimmering or giving its first wisp of smoke. Watch the oil the whole time to avoid a fire.
3. Pour the hot oil over the aromatics and mix.

## Used In

- [[Hainanese Chicken and Rice]]

Also great on: steamed chicken or fish, plain rice, noodles, roasted vegetables, eggs.
